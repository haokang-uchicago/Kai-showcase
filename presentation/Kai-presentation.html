Module C

Gestures

MediaPipe hands · USB camera

1  You say "Kai, what can I cook?"

Under the hood. The device on the fridge only talks to one place: our agent gateway on a Raspberry Pi. The gateway sends your voice to ElevenLabs for speech-to-text, asks DeepSeek which tool to call, reads the inventory module, and speaks the answer back. Gestures come from a USB camera on the server.
